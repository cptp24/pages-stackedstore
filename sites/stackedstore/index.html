  • Site search is the search box on stackedstore.com. Figures are visits (GA4 sessions) in the same 30 days as the Products tab, with visits from China left out.
  • Searches count visits that loaded the results page for a term. A visit that searched three terms counts once per term.
  • Product opens count visits that opened a product from the term, either from the results page or from the search dropdown. Text typed into the dropdown appears as its own term, which is why some terms show opens but no searches.
  • Basket adds count visits that added to basket from a product opened through search, or straight from the results page.
  • Stock is today's stock from the Products tab. A product marked No longer listed has been archived since.
  • Stock is the total across all locations, including both shops. Products that do not track inventory are listed under Not tracked.
  • Units sold are net of returns. Online Store counts the website only; All channels adds the shops (POS), the Shop app and draft orders.
  • Price is the lowest variant price. A struck-through price is the compare-at price.
  • Category comes from each product's tags in Shopify, matched to the website's menu. A product can sit in more than one category. No category means none of its tags matches a menu category.
  • In stock, missing from collection pages: products in stock (or not tracking stock) that are not on the Online Store, or that have show_in_collections off.
